wall-bound failures. Registry 64 records 0.177110152 seconds against bound
0.172732434; resource 16 records 0.184380192 against 0.183381580. All its RSS

Baseline/candidate closures are 220640984/321129728 bytes. This is a package

evaluator dependency. Merge and publish remain evaluator-free. TOML Kit retains
parsing, raw validation and retained-syntax merge under the approved fallback;
selected `fromTOML` and inspected alternatives did not qualify for protected
TOML 1.1 and concrete-syntax contracts. Pure parsing is not claimed impossible.

regression. Preserve raw failures and original bounds.
